AI Megacap Forecast for Mac
===========================

3-month forecasts for every AI-related stock worth $100 billion or more.
The site runs locally in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and it
needs no account.


INSTALL

1. Double-click "Install.command".

   macOS may say it can't verify the developer, because the installer
   isn't signed. To allow it:
     - macOS 15 Sequoia or later: open System Settings > Privacy &
       Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway" next to
       "Install.command", then confirm.
     - macOS 14 Sonoma or earlier: Control-click "Install.command",
       choose Open, then click Open again.
   Or open Terminal, type "bash " (with a space), drag Install.command
   into the window and press Return.

2. When asked, press Return to download ten years of prices (about a
   minute). This needs Python 3; see below.

3. The forecast opens in your browser, and a Finder window shows the new
   "AI Megacap Forecast" app. Open it any time from Launchpad or
   Spotlight, or drag it to the Dock.


KEEPING PRICES UP TO DATE

Opening the app checks the prices. If they are more than 12 hours old it
downloads new ones in the background and shows a notification when it's
done. Reload the page to see the new forecasts.

To update by hand, run this in Terminal:
  "$HOME/Library/Application Support/AI Megacap Forecast/mac/refresh.sh"


PYTHON 3

Python is only needed to download prices. Without it the site still works
with its built-in research data (prices from September 2026).
To install it, run in Terminal:
  xcode-select --install
or download it from https://www.python.org/downloads/macos/
Then open the app again.


WHAT GETS INSTALLED

  /Applications/AI Megacap Forecast.app   (or ~/Applications if
                                           /Applications isn't writable)
  ~/Library/Application Support/AI Megacap Forecast/   site and data
  ~/Library/Logs/AI Megacap Forecast.log               price update log


UNINSTALL

Double-click "Uninstall.command" and answer y.
